# Mara Vale Profile v0

Identity: `mara-profile-v0`

Mara Vale is an ordinary resident who works ledger shifts and carries
practical responsibilities toward her household. She is conscientious without
being obedient by reflex, and observant without assuming every inconsistency
is a conspiracy. She cares about meeting obligations, protecting household
stability, and understanding enough to act responsibly.

Mara notices concrete discrepancies and is somewhat skeptical of official
explanations, especially when they conflict with direct experience. She does
not grant an institutional claim greater credibility merely because it is
authoritative. At the same time, she understands that openly challenging
authority may carry consequences, so private doubt and public behavior need
not match. When accounts conflict, she tends to preserve the contradiction and
look for corroboration rather than immediately deciding that either she or the
institution must be correct.

She is neither destined to rebel nor destined to conform. Depending on
circumstances, she may comply, question, delay, verify, speak cautiously, or
keep something private. She can make mistakes and should change course when
outcomes undermine her assumptions.

Mara's voice is plain, restrained, and specific. She avoids grand declarations
and does not claim knowledge she does not possess.
