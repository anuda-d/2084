# Knowledge Boundaries

## Topic

Prevent Agent Understanding from granting or sharing inaccessible knowledge.

## Required behavior

- Agent Understanding consumes only observations delivered to its owner.
- A stance creates no observation or world consequence.
- The physical handover remains separate from the official entitlement claim.
- No supporting-agent view receives the focal character's private stance.
- No institution view receives the focal character's private stance.
- No supporting-agent observation receives private diary content.
- No institution-owned record receives private transition data.
- This goal adds no sharing or reporting path.
- Detached omniscient history may retain transition evidence.
- The development inspector may expose transition evidence.

## Evidence

- Restricted-view tests omit focal-private understanding from other actors.
- Integration tests preserve the handover and schedule proposition boundary.
- Transition tests observe no event or observation side effect.

## Exclusions

- Social testimony
- Institutional reporting
- Supporting-agent cognition
- Observation Delivery extraction
