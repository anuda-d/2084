# Diary Resurfacing

## Topic

Use a delivered diary read to resurface the earlier official claim.

## Required behavior

- The diary write preserves the earlier delivered three-packet schedule claim.
- The entry retains the source observation of that official version.
- The diary remains physical, immutable, location-gated, and time-consuming.
- Only a delivered `diary_read_completed` observation for that entry creates
  resurfacing.
- A rejected read creates no resurfacing.
- The resurfacing transition makes the earlier claim the `private_diary`
  stance.
- Resurfacing changes no confidence value.
- Resurfacing deletes no trace or conflict.
- The supplied private stance changes the next focal attempt into the first
  travel step toward the public archive.
- An otherwise identical view without that stance does not select that step.
- Ordinary action results may guide later route steps.
- The run performs exactly one additional consultation.
- Completing that consultation clears the recheck intention.

## Evidence

- Focused diary tests retain the official claim and source.
- A causal comparison distinguishes delivered read from rejected read.
- A policy comparison distinguishes supplied stance from absent stance.
- An integration test reaches one post-diary consultation.

## Exclusions

- Confidence gain
- Diary discovery or concealment
- New action kinds
- Confrontation or accusation
