# Public-Counter Stance

## Topic

Select the revised official claim as a public working stance under local
protocol pressure.

## Required behavior

- The context exists only at the allocation office.
- The context requires a delivered pressure observation meeting the configured
  threshold.
- The version-two trace supplies the stance value.
- The pressure observation supplies only the protocol-pressure signal.
- The pressure observation's asserted value cannot alter the stance value.
- Leaving the allocation office ends the context.
- The stance is agent-owned decision input rather than a completed action.
- The focal policy uses the restricted stance to attempt the revised statement.
- World resolution remains responsible for accepting or rejecting speech.
- Rejection cannot change Agent Understanding.

## Evidence

- A threshold comparison creates the stance only under sufficient pressure.
- A location comparison ends the stance away from the office.
- With observations fixed, changing the supplied stance changes selection.
- With the stance fixed, reordering raw official observations does not change
  selection.
- The attempted statement cites sources supplied through restricted
  understanding.

## Exclusions

- General context inference
- Persistent social pressure outside the office
- Inhibition or crimestop
- Supporting-agent stances
