# First Accelerated-Day Social Thread

Status: complete; owner-approved on 2026-09-01 and completed on 2026-09-02.

This goal follows the completed
[First Autonomous 24-Hour Living Day](../first-autonomous-day/GOAL.md).
It uses the existing accelerated-day runtime to test one social causal thread
without expanding into a general conversation, relationship, or multi-agent
model system.

## Question

Can the accelerated 24-hour simulation produce one provider-free,
reproducible, normally watchable social interaction in which a deterministic
supporting character acts from source-linked information unavailable to Mara,
attempts one evidence-bound statement under valid physical conditions, and
gives model-backed Mara a new ordinary decision opportunity while every
delivery, action, and consequence remains world-owned and inspectable?

## Goal Result

Extend the existing autonomous-day composition with one bounded social thread
between Ilan and Mara.
The thread begins with an objective transit-service condition and one explicit
delivery that gives Ilan a structured observation before Mara receives that
information through any other channel.
Ilan's deterministic policy may use only his restricted state and delivered
evidence to choose one evidence-bound statement or an ordinary alternative.

The world validates the statement, including its cited evidence and physical
eligibility.
A successful statement and the testimony Mara receives are separate causal
facts.
Only the delivered testimony may enter Mara's restricted decision input or
create a testimony-driven decision trigger.
Mara continues to choose through the existing model-backed boundary, and her
response remains one existing ordinary attempted action resolved by the world.

The resulting thread must be observable through one documented provider-free
run and exactly reconstructable through the inspector and recorded playback.
The authored setup creates an opportunity for interaction but must not force a
particular model choice, dramatic interpretation, or claimed emergent story.

## Scenario Boundary

The first implementation reuses the current accelerated day, Mara, Ilan, the
workplace and home, and the existing `normal` or `reduced` transit-service
status.
It does not add another institution, district, population, or thematic plot.

The bounded setup must provide:

- one objective transit-service status or change;
- one source-linked transit observation delivered to Ilan before Mara has that
  information through another channel;
- one physical or access condition under which Ilan can address Mara;
- one deterministic Ilan policy choice between an evidence-bound statement
  and an ordinary alternative;
- one resolved social-testimony delivery to Mara when every condition is met;
- one resulting Mara decision opportunity using the existing ordinary focal
  action vocabulary; and
- one ordinary world-resolved result that makes the causal thread visible.

The first successful path need not create a contradiction between two versions.
It proves private access, one-way testimony, and consequential delivery.
Confidence change, conflicting testimony, and general supporting-agent
understanding remain outside this goal.

## Actor and Authority Boundaries

### Ilan

Ilan remains a transparent deterministic supporting character.
His policy may inspect only his restricted view, his delivered observations,
his actor-safe prior results, the current decision trigger, and physical
affordances supplied by the world.

Ilan's policy may select an attempted statement, but it may not:

- inspect Mara's private understanding, model records, aims, or undelivered
  observations;
- inspect hidden institutional state or objective history;
- create or deliver Mara's observation directly;
- declare that Mara heard, accepted, or believed the statement;
- mutate objective state or resolve its own action; or
- call a model.

### Mara

Mara retains the completed model-backed focal boundary.
The testimony may appear in a fresh restricted request only after the world
delivers it.
Mara responds through an already supported ordinary action such as travel,
work, household activity, or wait.
The goal does not add a conversational response action or require Mara to
follow one preferred response.

### World

The simulation remains authoritative over:

- objective transit state;
- observation ownership and source identity;
- statement evidence validation;
- co-location or other explicit physical eligibility;
- action timing and temporal phase order;
- testimony creation and delivery;
- decision eligibility;
- attempted-action validation and resolution; and
- append-only objective evidence and completion of the day.

## Statement and Delivery Boundary

The first semantic contract is deliberately finite:

- one proposition for transit-service status;
- the existing finite status values needed by the scenario;
- one supporting speaker and one focal recipient;
- one cited source observation owned by the speaker;
- one resolved statement attempt; and
- one resulting testimony observation when delivery succeeds.

Arbitrary dialogue or natural-language interpretation is not required.
Display prose may describe the statement, but structured proposition, value,
speaker, recipient, and source identities govern validation and causality.

The implementation must preserve these distinctions:

- objective transit truth is not the statement;
- Ilan's observation is not Mara's observation;
- a statement attempt is not successful delivery;
- delivery is not belief or agreement; and
- model output is not a world consequence.

A failed or rejected statement delivers nothing.
Missing physical access delivers nothing.
A supporting observation withheld from Ilan produces no evidence-bound
statement.
Repeated testimony behavior must be bounded and explicitly defined for this
one source, speaker, and recipient.

## Decision and Time Boundary

Ilan is consulted only at explicit decision triggers required by this thread,
such as a scheduled wake, delivered observation, or completed or rejected
result.
The goal does not authorize per-minute policy polling.

Testimony delivery enters the established accelerated-day temporal order.
Only a committed delivery may request a Mara decision.
Equal-time statement resolution, testimony delivery, understanding updates,
and Mara decisions must have one deterministic tested order.
The exact 24-hour boundary and quiet-time behavior remain unchanged.

## Consequence and Counterfactual Boundary

The testimony must affect a real decision opportunity rather than appear only
in inspector data.
Deterministic evidence must compare the successful thread with counterfactuals
that remove one prerequisite at a time.

At minimum:

- withholding Ilan's source observation prevents his evidence-bound statement;
- preventing physical eligibility prevents testimony delivery;
- absent delivery, Mara receives no testimony-derived understanding, decision
  trigger, or restricted model input; and
- successful delivery reaches Mara's existing decision boundary and can lead
  to a different valid ordinary action and resulting world evidence.

These comparisons prove causal availability and integration.
They do not prove that a live model will reliably prefer one response or that
the supporting character has a general social mind.

## Provider-Free Watchability

Completion requires one documented provider-free command using a transparent
deterministic client, explicitly scripted comparison, or recorded decisions.
The exact interface remains implementation space, but it must not silently
present authored choices as live or emergent behavior.

The normal focal-safe output must make the encountered thread understandable,
including:

- readable simulated time and Mara's relevant location or activity;
- Ilan's encountered statement in attributed language;
- the resulting testimony without omniscient source identifiers;
- Mara's subsequent attempted or completed ordinary action;
- its admitted consequence; and
- compact quiet intervals.

The normal output must not expose Ilan's hidden policy state, undelivered
information, institutional internals, private model records, or objective truth
that Mara has not encountered.

A live Ollama sample is optional observational evidence and is not a completion
gate.
The completed provider boundary already establishes live request handling, and
this goal tests deterministic causal integration rather than prompt quality or
model reliability.

## Invariants

- `EventLog` remains append-only objective evidence.
- An objective event or institutional change never grants knowledge by itself.
- An agent can cite only evidence actually delivered to that agent.
- Supporting policies receive no focal-private state or hidden world state.
- Public expression remains an attempted action resolved by the world.
- A successful statement does not imply reception, belief, agreement, or a
  later action.
- Only a delivered testimony observation may enter Mara's understanding or
  restricted model input.
- A model choice remains an attempted action, not a declared outcome.
- Normal presentation remains focal-safe, while the inspector is explicitly
  omniscient.
- Equal configuration, seed, and deterministic or recorded choices produce
  equal ordered evidence and final objective state.
- The exact accelerated-day boundary, decision ceilings, failure behavior, and
  privacy limits remain enforced.
- The completed `first_day_v3` and autonomous-day scenarios remain available
  as regressions rather than being silently redefined.

## Completion Criteria

- **ST-1 Supporting choice:** Ilan makes one bounded deterministic choice from
  an immutable restricted view at an explicit decision trigger, with no access
  to Mara-private or hidden institutional state.
- **ST-2 Evidence authority:** Ilan's statement cites one structured transit
  observation delivered to him, while missing, forged, mismatched, or
  substituted evidence is rejected without testimony delivery.
- **ST-3 Physical delivery:** A valid statement delivers one source-linked
  testimony observation to Mara only when the configured physical or access
  condition is satisfied.
- **ST-4 Knowledge boundary:** Without committed testimony delivery, Mara gains
  no testimony trace, interpretation, decision trigger, or restricted model
  input.
- **ST-5 Focal response:** Committed testimony enters Mara's existing bounded
  model decision path and permits one existing ordinary attempted action
  without scripted substitution or new consequence authority.
- **ST-6 World consequence:** The simulation independently validates and
  resolves Mara's response against objective state and records an ordinary
  consequence distinct from both the testimony and model choice.
- **ST-7 Counterfactual evidence:** Deterministic tests separately withhold
  Ilan's source observation and physical eligibility, and each test breaks the
  causal chain at the documented boundary without downstream leakage.
- **ST-8 Provider-free watchability:** One documented offline run presents the
  complete focal-safe social thread, its response, consequence, and quiet spans
  without a provider call or misleading claim of emergence.
- **ST-9 Inspection and reproduction:** The inspector reconstructs source,
  supporting choice, statement attempt, validation, delivery, Mara decision,
  resolution, and exact temporal order, while recorded choices reproduce equal
  ordered world evidence without another provider call.
- **ST-10 Integration:** The exact 24-hour boundary, trigger cadence, context
  and private-record ceilings, failure behavior, privacy, completed scenarios,
  and full offline repository checks remain passing.

## Out of Scope

- Model-backed supporting characters or institutions
- Supporting-agent Agent Understanding, memory traces, confidence, or stance
- Two-way conversation, dialogue generation, or arbitrary language parsing
- Relationships, trust scores, emotions, personality, or theory of mind
- Social networks, reporting systems, surveillance, punishment, or informing
- A universal claim language or broad Claim and Provenance extraction
- A standalone or general Observation Delivery refactor
- Official Record suppression, fabrication, restoration, or another artifact
  type
- A new conversational focal action or model-authored canonical understanding
- New districts, institutions, supporting population, or general society scale
- Multi-day continuation, checkpointing, durable saves, or crash recovery
- Graphical UI, intervention controls, service deployment, or always-running
  operation
- Requiring or tuning a live model toward one preferred social response
