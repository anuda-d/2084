# First Consequential Choice Under Conflicting Information

Status: active; owner-approved goal on 2026-09-06.

This goal follows the completed [First Accelerated-Day Social Thread](../first-accelerated-day-social-thread/GOAL.md).
It connects the existing accelerated day, source-linked understanding, restricted model decisions, and social testimony around an ordinary choice with physical consequences.

## Question

Can Mara encounter conflicting official information and personal testimony about the same transit service interval, choose how to handle competing work and household obligations, and experience a world-resolved outcome that informs her next decision?

## Goal Result

Within one accelerated 24-hour day, Mara encounters one finite official transit claim and one incompatible statement from Ilan through legitimate information access.
Both claims concern the same route and service interval, and her understanding retains both sources and their conflict.
At a relevant decision opportunity, at least two physically feasible ordinary actions have different consequences for her work and household obligations.
Mara's model chooses one of those feasible competing-obligation alternatives as an attempted action from her restricted state.
The world resolves travel against actual service conditions and resolves obligations against elapsed time, location, completed activity, and configured deadlines.
Mara receives the resulting consequence through a valid observation or actor-safe result and gets a subsequent decision opportunity.

The normal focal-safe presentation must make the encountered accounts, choice, consequence, and follow-through understandable.
Deterministic comparisons must establish the separate effects of information, choice, and physical conditions, and recorded playback must reproduce the ordered evidence.
One reviewed live-model day must exercise the conflict-to-consequence boundary without requiring a preferred choice or dramatic outcome.

## Scenario Boundary

Keep the composition to one day, Mara, deterministic Ilan, the existing transit institution, and existing locations.
Use the existing finite transit statuses, such as `normal` and `reduced`, for one route and one explicitly identified service interval.
Use Mara's existing work and household obligations and ordinary action vocabulary, including travel, work, household activity, and wait.

The bounded scenario may configure starting circumstances, access opportunities, official publication and delivery times, Ilan's source delivery, service-dependent travel durations, activity durations, and obligation deadlines.
These values are implementation choices inside this goal when documented as authored inputs and supported by focused evidence.
They must create a real tradeoff and an opportunity to encounter it without secretly selecting Mara's actions.
No particular branch, missed obligation, belief, or emotional interpretation is a required live-model outcome.

The new behavior must have a documented configuration or command that preserves the completed `first_day_v3`, autonomous-day, and social-comparison scenarios as regressions.
Do not silently redefine a completed demonstration to make the new goal appear satisfied.

## Information and Conflict Boundary

The official account is a finite structured claim from the existing institutional/Official Record boundary.
Ilan's account cites a valid source observation actually delivered to him and reaches Mara only through a physically valid, world-resolved testimony delivery.
Mara retains each account's source, asserted status, route, and service interval.
A change of service at two different times does not by itself satisfy the conflict criterion.
The two incompatible claims must apply to the same interval relevant to her choice.

Official publication, objective service state, Ilan's source observation, his attempted statement, and Mara's delivered testimony remain distinct causal facts.
The world may hold a condition different from the official account without granting the institution an authored intent to lie.
Mara's understanding may identify incompatibility but may not consult hidden objective truth to choose the correct source.
Delivery does not imply belief, agreement, a trust score, or obedience.
No evidence may be erased or overwritten to resolve the conflict.

Keep any extension to claim matching and understanding finite and limited to this proposition.
General language interpretation, supporting-agent cognition, a relationship system, and extraction of general observation or provenance frameworks are outside this goal.

## Choice and Consequence Boundary

At the relevant decision point, Mara has at least two feasible alternatives with different possible obligation outcomes, such as leaving for home or spending more time at work.
The available alternatives, known obligations, and deadlines must be accessible through her legitimate state or delivered information.
The restricted model input must expose the encountered conflict without revealing undelivered institutional state, Ilan's private source observation, or the world's hidden transit status.

Actual transit conditions must affect physical travel duration or availability.
The same accepted travel attempt under different objective service conditions must have a demonstrably different physical result independent of what Mara was told.
In the documented comparison, that physical difference must cross an explicit obligation deadline and change at least one fulfilled/missed status while delivered information and the comparable action path remain fixed.
Information alone never changes the transit service or declares arrival.

The world determines whether each bounded obligation is fulfilled or missed using explicit deadlines and actual completed activity at the required location.
An arrival may enable household activity but is not automatically completed household time.
Boundary behavior at a deadline, pending activity, and any service change during travel must be defined and tested for the supported configuration.
Consequences remain limited to elapsed time, location, and the status of these obligations.
Do not add wages, punishment, health damage, relationship changes, or a wider economy to make the result feel consequential.

Preserve the established non-interruption boundary for pending actions.
New information may be retained while Mara is busy and become available at her next legitimate decision opportunity.
Follow-through must use her delivered consequence and retained understanding; it does not require a second conversation or a particular repair action.

## Completion Criteria

- **CC-1 Shared referent and conflict:** Two delivered, source-linked incompatible transit claims concern the same route and service interval, and Mara retains both plus an explicit conflict without hidden truth access or source erasure.
- **CC-2 Legitimate access:** Official publication and delivery remain separate, Ilan speaks only from his delivered source, and missing source evidence or physical access prevents testimony and downstream knowledge.
- **CC-3 Feasible tradeoff:** One documented configuration exposes at least two feasible ordinary choices with different work/household obligation outcomes, using agent-accessible constraints and explicit authored timings.
- **CC-4 Autonomous choice:** Committed deliveries reach the existing restricted model decision boundary in deterministic temporal order, and the model selects one of the documented CC-3 competing-obligation alternatives as its ordinary attempted action without scripted substitution or a prescribed branch.
- **CC-5 Physical transit effect:** Actual service conditions affect the duration or availability of an otherwise equivalent travel attempt independently of the delivered accounts, and this physical difference changes at least one deadline-governed fulfilled/missed obligation status under the comparable action path, with documented resolution and pending-action rules.
- **CC-6 Obligation outcomes:** Explicit world rules use actual location, completed activity, and deadlines to record fulfilled or missed obligations, including focused deadline-boundary evidence and no success from arrival alone.
- **CC-7 Follow-through:** Mara receives an attributable consequence and a subsequent legitimate decision opportunity containing that result and retained conflict, without hidden outcome knowledge or forced corrective behavior.
- **CC-8 Causal comparisons:** Deterministic evidence separately varies delivered information with the world held fixed, selected actions with information and world held fixed, and objective transit conditions with delivered information and the comparable action path held fixed; the documented differences occur at the appropriate knowledge, choice, and physical boundaries, and the physical comparison changes at least one obligation's fulfilled/missed status through its deadline.
- **CC-9 Provider-free watchability:** A documented offline run shows the encountered conflicting accounts, attempted action, actual travel result, obligation outcome, subsequent action opportunity or attempt, and compact quiet spans through a focal-safe normal surface that identifies authored or recorded decisions honestly.
- **CC-10 Inspection and replay:** Inspection reconstructs the ordered source-to-conflict-to-choice-to-consequence chain, and recorded decisions reproduce equal ordered events, observations, relevant understanding, final objective state, and decision statuses without another provider call.
- **CC-11 Reviewed live day:** One bounded live run through the existing local model integration reaches the conflict-informed decision boundary, selects one of the documented CC-3 competing-obligation alternatives, produces its world-resolved obligation consequence, delivers that result into a follow-up decision, reaches the exact day boundary, and retains a reviewed private audit and reproducible recorded evidence; no particular alternative or obligation outcome is required.
- **CC-12 Integration and completion:** Focused behavioral and counterfactual checks, existing scenario regressions, privacy/cadence/growth/failure checks, `./scripts/check.sh`, and a final fresh independent whole-goal review pass without weakening existing boundaries.
