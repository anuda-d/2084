# Core Construct

Status: current product direction; worldbuilding and final presentation remain open.
2084 follows one autonomous person inside a small social world shaped by institutions, limited information, ordinary obligations, and conflicting accounts.
The central experience is watching an understandable life unfold while the wider world continues beyond that person's attention.

## Focal life

Mara is an embodied participant, not a privileged narrator or player-controlled source of truth.
Location, time, needs, obligations, delivered evidence, and prior outcomes constrain what she can do.
Her special role is presentational: the observer follows her more closely.

The normal experience centers on autonomous watching and understandable consequences.
It does not require conventional game objectives, victory conditions, population statistics, or approval of every action.
A supporting schedule or institutional event matters when it can affect a later choice or encounter.
Do not add a larger society merely to imply scale.

## Truth, claims, and expression

Preserve the distinctions between what happened, what an institution publishes, what a person encountered, what they retain or conclude, and what they publicly attempt to express.
Objective history never changes because an official account changes.
A revised record grants no automatic knowledge and does not rewrite uncontrolled copies, observations, or memories.
Institutions need authority, access, evidence, and any modeled time or capacity.
They can be powerful while remaining incomplete and fallible.

Doublethink-inspired behavior should use source-linked claims, explicit contradictions, confidence, contextual stance, accessibility, and inspectable inhibition or resurfacing.
A conflict must not erase source evidence or become a single loyalty score.
Public expression remains an attempted action, distinct from private or contextual understanding and resolved separately by the world.
These mechanisms do not claim to reproduce consciousness.

Begin with finite, understandable propositions such as entitlement amounts and transit service intervals.
General language interpretation, memory decay, trust, and motivated reinterpretation require their own behavioral justification and approved scope.
Claim/provenance and observation-delivery modules should be extracted only when a concrete repeated responsibility needs them.

## Model and world

In model mode, Mara's structured choice becomes her actual attempted action.
The world owns truth, eligibility, access, action validity, duration, and consequence.
The model cannot inspect hidden state, create canonical observations or memories, advance time, or declare success.
Identity and continuity remain explicit simulation state rather than opaque provider conversation history.

Supporting characters and institutions remain deterministic.
Simple rules are preferable when they answer the same question more clearly.
Provider failures remain explicit safe waits; the scripted focal policy is a separately selected comparison, never a hidden fallback.
A successful model sample establishes only the boundary exercised, not general competence, human fidelity, or deterministic live behavior.

## Diary and physical consequence

The diary is a physical object with possession or location requirements.
Reading and writing consume time; entries retain Mara's perspective and source evidence.
Discovery, concealment, confiscation, and sharing should be added only when they create a necessary interaction.
The diary must earn its role through behavior rather than thematic recognition.

Consequences should have understandable world causes, including delays and failed attempts.
Authored starting circumstances and schedules may create a tradeoff but must not secretly select the focal character's response.
Do not force a rebellion, betrayal, or punishment sequence and describe it as emergence.

## Observer direction

The implemented normal surfaces are read-only terminal views.
They show Mara's admitted encounters and actions; a visibly separate development inspector exposes objective causal evidence.
A graphical interface, pause controls, playback speed, history browser, and intervention remain unimplemented possibilities.

A future interface should make the focal person, current activity, location, recent consequence, and next uncertainty easy to follow.
Narration must come from simulation records rather than inventing connective events.
Official accounts should remain attributed claims, and the diary should remain an earlier personal perspective.
Contradictions should be understandable through encountered sources rather than automatically reduced to scores or truth labels.
Any future intervention must be explicit and cannot grant impossible knowledge or silently replace Mara's agency.

## Current scope

The repository implements official-record revision, source-linked understanding and diary resurfacing, optional model-backed Mara, exact 24-hour execution, and one deterministic social-testimony chain.
The active [consequential-choice goal](../plans/first-consequential-choice/GOAL.md) adds conflicting transit accounts and a work/household tradeoff.
Its accepted implementation and remaining live-choice evidence are recorded in [implementation state](../plans/first-consequential-choice/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
[Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) describes the implemented boundaries; it does not promise a general society, persistent mind, durable world, or human realism.

## References and limits

[Nineteen Eighty-Four](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/09/Nineteen-Eighty-Four.pdf) informs the themes of uncertain surveillance, self-censorship, mutable official accounts, public/private divergence, and contradiction.
Borrow mechanisms, not Orwell's characters, exact institutions, setting, plot, or terminal punishment arc.
Graphic torture, global war simulation, and a full language-control system are outside the current prototype.

[Agentic World Modeling, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22748v1) is a source of architectural and evaluation ideas: partial observability, separate decision and transition authority, explicit constraints, retained failures, and reproduction.
It is not evidence that 2084 models human behavior accurately or justification for large populations, self-revising social models, or digital-twin claims.
Evaluation infrastructure should support the focal experience rather than become the normal interface.

The local reference project `/Users/anuda/Desktop/sim` supplied the concept of watchable agency: one autonomous focal person, time and place constraints, independently advancing events, and compact explanations.
Borrow those concepts without copying its code, characters, scenario, interface, or authored objectives.
Consult its actual implementation before relying on a claimed feature.

## Design test and open questions

A proposed system should change a decision, interaction, uncertainty, or consequence the observer can understand.
Prefer the simplest mechanism that preserves limited knowledge, bounded authority, and inspectable causes.
Remove systems that obscure the central question.

Open decisions include the lasting setting and cast, the first additional institutional limit, meaningful supporting autonomy, which skill changes repeated model failures justify, the amount of private state shown to the observer, the value of bounded intervention, and the eventual spatial presentation.
These are open design space, not an implementation queue.
